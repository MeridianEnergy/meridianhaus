Information We May Collect

When you use this website or contact Meridian Haus, you may voluntarily provide information such as your name, email address, telephone number, company or organisation name, event details, guest numbers, venue information, catering preferences and other information included in your enquiry.

We may also receive technical information associated with your visit, such as browser type, device information, approximate location, referring website and website usage information, depending on the services and analytics tools active on the website.

02

Information submitted through the website may be used to respond to enquiries, prepare catering quotations, understand event requirements, communicate with prospective or existing clients and provide requested services.

Catering Enquiries

Information provided through a catering quote request is used to understand the requirements of the proposed event and to communicate with the person making the enquiry.

Submitting an enquiry does not automatically create a booking or guarantee availability. A catering booking only becomes confirmed when Meridian Haus and the client have agreed to the relevant arrangements.

If you provide contact information, Meridian Haus may use it to respond to your enquiry or provide information directly related to your request.
