Rogue Recipe #001

The Hellraiser

A seriously loaded burger created for people who believe a burger should never be boring.

The Rogue Chef

The Burger

The Hellraiser is built around the idea that a great burger should be generous, messy, bold and packed with flavour.

This is the kind of burger that doesn't apologise for being a little excessive.

Rogue Kitchen Note

Don't rush the cooking. Give the burger time to develop colour and flavour, then build it while everything is hot.

Ingredients

  • 250 g beef mince
  • 1 burger roll
  • 1 slice or portion of cheese
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion
  • Pickles
  • Burger sauce of your choice
  • Salt
  • Black pepper
  • Cooking oil

Method

  1. Shape the beef mince into a burger patty, keeping the mixture together without overworking it.
  2. Season both sides generously with salt and freshly ground black pepper.
  3. Heat a suitable pan or cooking surface and add a small amount of cooking oil.
  4. Cook the patty until properly browned on the outside and cooked to your preferred level of doneness.
  5. Add the cheese toward the end of cooking and allow it to melt over the patty.
  6. Lightly toast the inside of the burger roll.
  7. Add your sauce, lettuce, tomato, onion and pickles to the roll.
  8. Place the hot burger patty into the roll, finish the build and serve immediately.

Make It Yours

Add your favourite toppings, change the sauce or turn up the heat. The Rogue Chef kitchen is about experimenting and finding your own version.
