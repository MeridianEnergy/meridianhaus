ROGUE RECIPE #001

The Hellraiser.

A burger built for people who don't believe dinner should be boring.

🍔 BURGER 🔥 ROGUE ORIGINAL ⏱ APPROX. 30 MIN

THE STORY

This Isn't Just A Burger.

The Hellraiser was created around one simple idea: take the humble burger and turn the flavour dial all the way up.

It's rich, bold, messy and completely unapologetic. This is the kind of burger you build when you aren't interested in playing it safe.

Welcome to Rogue Recipe #001.

SERVES 1
PREP 15 MIN
COOK 15 MIN
LEVEL EASY

WHAT YOU NEED

Ingredients

The Burger

  • 1 quality burger bun
  • 250 g beef mince
  • Salt, to taste
  • Black pepper, to taste
  • 1 slice cheese
  • Lettuce
  • Tomato
  • Onion

Rogue Sauce

  • 2 tbsp mayonnaise
  • 1 tbsp tomato sauce
  • 1 tsp mustard
  • Hot sauce, to taste
  • Pinch of smoked paprika

Optional Extras

  • Crispy bacon
  • Jalapeños
  • Caramelised onions
  • Extra cheese

LET'S COOK

Method

01

Make The Sauce

Combine the mayonnaise, tomato sauce, mustard, hot sauce and smoked paprika. Mix well and set aside.

02

Shape The Patty

Gently shape the beef mince into a burger patty. Avoid overworking the meat.

Season both sides generously with salt and black pepper.

03

Cook The Beef

Heat a heavy pan or grill until hot. Cook the burger until browned and cooked to your preferred level of doneness.

04

Add The Cheese

Place the cheese over the burger near the end of cooking and allow it to melt.

05

Toast The Bun

Lightly toast the cut sides of the bun in the same pan for extra flavour.

06

Build The Hellraiser

Spread the Rogue Sauce over the toasted bun. Add lettuce, tomato, onion and the burger patty.

Add any optional extras you want and finish with another layer of sauce.

07

Serve

Serve immediately while the burger is hot, juicy and gloriously messy.

ROGUE CHEF TIP

Don't Flatten The Flavour.

Don't press the burger repeatedly while it's cooking. Let the surface develop a proper crust and keep the juices where they belong — inside the burger.

ROGUE ON A BUDGET

Make It Your Own.

You don't need expensive ingredients to make a memorable burger. Use the beef you have available, choose a good basic bun and focus your money on the flavours that matter most.

Leftover roast meat, homemade sauces and simple toppings can also turn an ordinary burger night into something seriously good.
