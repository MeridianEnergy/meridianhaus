WHO WE ARE

Built Around Great Food.

Meridian Haus is a modern catering house built around one simple idea: food should do more than fill a plate. It should create atmosphere, bring people together and become part of the memory of an occasion.

We create catering experiences for private celebrations, corporate functions, weddings, birthdays, office events, memorial gatherings and everything in between.

Every event is different, which is why we believe catering should never feel completely off-the-shelf. Menus can be shaped around the occasion, the guests, the budget and the experience you want to create.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Simple Principles.
Serious Food.

Great catering doesn't need to be complicated. It needs to be considered.

01

Flavour First

Everything starts with flavour. Ingredients, preparation and presentation should work together to create food people genuinely want to eat.

02

Made For The Moment

A corporate breakfast and a wedding celebration are completely different experiences. Our approach starts with understanding the occasion.

03

Creativity Matters

We aren't afraid to experiment. New combinations, unexpected flavours and fresh ideas are part of what makes the kitchen interesting.

04

Hospitality Always

Catering is ultimately about people. Professional service, communication and attention to detail are just as important as what's on the plate.

OUR APPROACH

From The First Conversation.

Good catering starts long before the food arrives.

We begin by understanding what you're planning: the type of event, number of guests, timing, venue, style of service and the kind of food you want.

From there, we can help shape a menu and catering approach that makes sense for your event.

Whether you need a simple office lunch or a complete event catering experience, the goal remains the same: make the food one less thing you need to worry about.
